## Sample Questions

### Question 1: Self-Introduction
- **Title (JP)**: 自己紹介をお願いします。
- **Title (EN)**: Please introduce yourself.
- **Category**: Self-Introduction
- **Tips**: Keep it under 1-2 minutes. Mention your name, nationality, current status (student/worker), a brief summary of your skills, and end with the phrase "Yoroshiku onegaishimasu".
- **Common Mistakes**: Summarizing your entire resume, talking too long, or being too informal.

### Question 2: Why Japan
- **Title (JP)**: なぜ日本で働きたいですか？
- **Title (EN)**: Why do you want to work in Japan?
- **Category**: Motivation / Why Japan
- **Tips**: Show genuine interest in Japanese culture, but also tie it strictly to professional growth (e.g., respect for Japanese engineering, market size, specific company reputation).
- **Common Mistakes**: Saying "I like anime" without providing any professional career-oriented reasons.

### Question 3: IT Engineering
- **Title (JP)**: 今までで一番難しかった開発経験を教えてください。
- **Title (EN)**: What is the most difficult development experience you've had?
- **Category**: IT / Technical
- **Tips**: Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result). Focus heavily on the Action you took to resolve the technical issue.
- **Common Mistakes**: Blaming your team members for the difficulty, or explaining the project without explaining your specific contribution.
